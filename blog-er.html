Signs You Should Rush to the Emergency Room

Most illnesses can wait for a clinic appointment in the morning. A few cannot wait even thirty minutes. Knowing the difference can save a life, and it can also save you an anxious night of guessing.

Come Immediately If You See These

These signs point to problems where minutes genuinely matter. Do not drive yourself if you can avoid it. Call us on +254 745 190190 or get someone to bring you straight to our doors on Muyuyu Avenue.

  • Chest pain or pressure that lasts more than a few minutes, especially if it spreads to the arm, jaw or back.
  • Difficulty breathing or gasping for air at rest.
  • Heavy bleeding that does not stop with steady direct pressure.
  • Sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body, slurred speech, or a drooping face. These are stroke signs.
  • Loss of consciousness, a seizure, or confusion that came on suddenly.
  • Severe injuries from road accidents, deep cuts, serious falls, or burns larger than the palm of a hand.
  • A baby under three months with any fever at all.

What To Do in the First Minutes

Stay as calm as you can, because your calm transfers to the patient. If someone is bleeding, press firmly on the wound with the cleanest cloth available and keep pressing. If someone is unconscious but breathing, roll them gently onto their side. If you suspect a spine or neck injury from a fall or crash, do not move the person unless there is immediate danger like fire.

Bring any medication the patient is taking, and a list of allergies if you know it. If the patient is a child, bring their immunization booklet. These small details help our team act faster.

If you are ever unsure whether something is an emergency, treat it as one. No one at Northlight will ever scold you for coming in and being fine. That is exactly the outcome we hope for.

What Can Safely Wait Until Morning

Mild colds, small rashes without fever, a sprained ankle you can still walk on, toothache without facial swelling, and routine medication refills can all wait for a normal clinic slot. If you are unsure, call first and describe the symptoms. Our staff will give you an honest answer about coming now or booking in.

We Are Ready When You Are

Our accident and emergency department runs every hour of every day and night, with ICU and HDU beds, on site CT, X-ray and laboratory, so the answers come to you instead of sending you across town. Save our number in your phone today, before you ever need it.
